How to use Coffee Dial

Capture every cup

Use Coffee dial to capture your brews, log coffees you tasted and keep stock of your beans.

1. Log your brews

Tap the plus icon in the header to capture a new brew. Fill in bean details, extraction metrics and cupping notes. Sort, filter and edit past brews in the brews table.

2. Rating your brews

Use the star rating system to track which brews you loved and which ones need refinement. This helps you identify your best recipes at a glance.

3. Opened bags

Use the Opened bags section above the brew table to see at a glance which coffee beans are currently available. From there, you choose from the highest rated brews per method for that coffee to repeat and adjust or fast repeat the brew.

4. Moments & sharing

Share brew moments with friends, including brew details and graphs when available. Use the Moments view to browse My moments and Friends moments, comment, and share moments outside the app.

5. Statistics & analytics

View detailed brewing statistics over time. See charts on methods, roasters, and trends to guide your next experiment.

6. Connect your scale

Connect a compatible scale to capture weight and flow live while brewing. See pours and swirls in the graph with data per item, and record key moments like first drip, max flow, and AVG flow.

7. Lab results

Use Lab results to compare brews and inspect relationships between variables on custom graphs. Select brews and axes to analyze what actually improves your cup.

8. Share & follow friends

Follow other brewers, react, and join discussions on moments. Sharing with friends is a fast way to learn new recipes and techniques.

9. Keep stock of your beans

Use the Beans menu to track open, frozen, and finished bags. Beans available per bag is automatically recalculated after every brew so you always know what is available. After finishing a bag it is archived.

10. Navigation & gestures

Double-tap any brew row to open the full brew card. Use the menu icon () for actions like editing, duplicating, or deleting.

11. AI integration

Use AI to scan coffee bag labels and auto-fill roaster, origin, and roast details. You can also analyze your profile to discover trends in your preferences.
