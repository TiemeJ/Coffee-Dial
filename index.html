How to Use Coffee Dial

Your Brew, Your Way

Coffee Dial is flexible. You can use it to log every single shot you make to track consistency, OR you can just save your favourite recipes (Golden Cups) and update them as you dial in a new bean. It's your laboratory!

1. Adding & Editing Brews

Tap the "Add New Brew" bar at the top to open the form. Fill in your details (Roaster, Grind, Ratio, etc.). You can use the SCA Flavor Wheel for notes! To edit, simply tap the icon on any row.

2. Pinning & Active Brews

Found a recipe you love? Click the Pin icon () to move it to the "Pinned Brews" tiles. This keeps your daily drivers separated from your experiment history.

3. Rating Your Brews

Use the star rating system to track which brews you loved and which ones need refinement. Hover over the stars to rate, or click them to set your rating. This helps you quickly identify your best recipes at a glance.

4. Coffee Photos & Gallery

Capture and attach photos of your brews to track visual consistency and color development. Browse the Coffee Gallery to see your brew journey over time and share beautiful shots with friends. Your coffee deserves to be seen!

5. Statistics & Analytics

View detailed brewing statistics to analyze your brew data over time. See charts on brewing methods, roasters, and more. Let data guide your next experiment and discover patterns in your perfect cup.

6. Share & Follow Friends

Share your brew cards and coffee photos with friends directly from the app. Follow other brewers to see their experiments and get inspired. Building a community is the best part of the coffee journey!

7. Beans Functionality

Access the Beans menu from the main navigation to keep track of which bags you currently have open. Record and update your stock levels to monitor how much of each bean you have left. Never run out of your favorite coffee unexpectedly!

8. Navigation & Gestures

Double-tap any brew row to quickly open the full coffee card view. Use the menu icon () for actions like editing, duplicating, or deleting. Swipe through your pinned brews for quick access to your daily drivers.

9. AI Integration

Use AI-powered features to scan coffee bag labels and automatically extract roaster, origin, and roast level information. The AI can also analyze your coffee profile to understand what kind of coffee drinker you are based on all your brews, helping you discover new preferences and brewing styles.

About Coffee Dial

This app is made by two amateur brewers, Tieme & Robert, who wanted some method to their madness of home coffee experimentation.

Feel free to spread our love for coffee and share the link to this app with friends and follow each other's brews.
